What a numerology calculator does

Numerology is a traditional practice that gives each number from 1 to 9 (and the master numbers 11, 22 and 33) its own character, then turns your birth date and name into a handful of those numbers. This calculator does the adding for you and shows every step, so you can check it by hand.

To be straightforward with you: there is no scientific evidence that numbers can predict your personality, relationships, health, money or future. What numerology does well is give you a short, structured set of questions to think about. Take what feels true, leave the rest, and treat it as a prompt for reflection, not an answer.

How to calculate your life path number by hand

Your life path number comes from your full date of birth. Reduce the month, the day and the year on their own, add the three results, then reduce the total. Keep 11, 22 and 33 when they appear.

Worked example for 14 July 1990:

  • Month: July is 7.
  • Day: 14 becomes 1 + 4 = 5.
  • Year: 1990 becomes 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19, then 1 + 9 = 10, then 1 + 0 = 1.
  • Total: 7 + 5 + 1 = 13, then 1 + 3 = 4.

So the life path number is 4.

Master numbers: 11, 22 and 33

If you land on 11, 22 or 33, numerologists keep it as a master number instead of reducing it. They usually describe 11 as a louder 2 (intuition), 22 as a 4 with a bigger vision (building), and 33 as a 6 with a wider reach (care and teaching). Many people read both the master number and its single-digit form. 33 is the least commonly used.

Your personal year, and 2027

A personal year is a nine-year cycle that numerology uses to describe the tone of each year for you. Add your birth month, your birth day and the year you want, reducing each to a single digit first, then reduce the total to one digit. For 2027 the year part is 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, then 1 + 1 = 2.

Here is what each personal year traditionally stands for. Many people read their number at the start of January and again in December, as a gentle way to plan the next twelve months.

Name numbers explained

Name numbers give each letter a value from 1 to 9 using the Pythagorean chart (A, J and S are 1; B, K and T are 2; and so on down to I and R, which are 9). Add the values, reduce the total, and you have a number.

  • Expression (or destiny) number: all the letters in your full birth name. Linked with your natural talents and way of working.
  • Soul urge number: only the vowels (A, E, I, O, U). Linked with what you care about at heart. We count Y as a consonant.
  • Personality number: only the consonants. Linked with the first impression you give.

Questions

Is numerology real?

There is no scientific evidence that numbers predict personality or the future. Numerology is a traditional belief system, not a science. Many people still enjoy it as a prompt for reflection: the meanings are just a starting point for asking yourself useful questions.

Do I use my birth name or my current name?

Traditional numerology uses the full name on your birth certificate for the expression, soul urge and personality numbers. Some people also try the name they use today to see how it differs. Your life path number comes only from your date of birth, so it never changes.

What are master numbers?

11, 22 and 33 are called master numbers. When a total comes out as one of them, numerologists usually keep it instead of reducing it further, and describe it as a stronger version of 2, 4 or 6. Many numerologists use only 11 and 22, and some people prefer to read both the master number and its single-digit form.

How is a personal year calculated?

Add your birth month, your birth day and the year you want to look at, reducing each to a single digit first, then reduce the total to one digit. For 2027, someone born on 14 July does 7 + (1 + 4) + (2 + 0 + 2 + 7), which gives 7 + 5 + 2 = 14, then 1 + 4 = 5. That is a personal year 5.

Why does my life path number differ on another site?

Different sites use slightly different rules, mainly about when to keep master numbers. This calculator reduces your month, day and year separately and keeps 11, 22 and 33 whenever they appear, then adds them and keeps a master number if the total is one. Our angel number page uses the same method, so both pages give the same number.

Is my name or birthday saved?

No. Everything is worked out in your browser. Your name and date of birth are never sent or saved anywhere.
