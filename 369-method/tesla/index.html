Did Nikola Tesla really create the 369 method?

Almost every 369 method video opens with Nikola Tesla. The truth is less magical but more useful: Tesla did not invent a journaling method, and the practice works for ordinary reasons that have nothing to do with him.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Tejas Rane, maker of 369 Journal and author of two books on manifestation journaling

The quote everyone shares

The line usually credited to Tesla says that if you only knew the magnificence of 3, 6 and 9, you would have a key to the universe. It appears on countless posters, videos and app pages.

There is no reliable source for it. It has not been found in Tesla’s verified writings, and the posts that share it never give a date, letter, lecture or interview. The fact-checking site Truth or Fiction found no trace of it before about 2018, decades after his death in 1943, and rates it as misattributed.

What Tesla really did with the number 3

Tesla did have a well-known fondness for the number three. His biographer Margaret Cheney described how he asked for 18 napkins at meals because the number was divisible by three (PBS interview). Other, less well sourced stories say he walked around a block three times before going into a building and preferred hotel room numbers divisible by three. These read as personal habits, not a spiritual system.

None of these accounts mention writing an affirmation 3, 6 and 9 times a day. That connection was made by others, decades later.

Where the 369 method actually came from

The 369 method as people practise it today became popular through law of attraction communities and TikTok around 2020 and 2021. Creators combined an older idea, writing a desire repeatedly as a daily ritual, with Tesla’s name and his supposed quote, which gave it a memorable story. It spread quickly because it is simple, free and easy to share.

Does the Tesla story matter?

Not much. What helps people is the structure, not the name behind it:

  • Three sessions a day keep your goal in mind morning, afternoon and night instead of once a year.
  • Writing by hand or typing slowly makes you read your goal properly.
  • A fixed length (21, 33 or 45 days) gives the habit a clear finish line.
  • A short note each night helps you notice small progress you would otherwise forget.

We don’t use Tesla to sell our app or books, and we don’t promise that writing an affirmation makes things happen by itself. We think the 369 method is worth doing as a calm daily focus habit, which it is, whoever came up with it.

Questions

Did Nikola Tesla invent the 369 method?

No. There is no record of Tesla creating or describing a journaling method. The 369 method became popular online around 2020 and 2021, and his name was attached to it later.

Did Tesla say 3, 6 and 9 are the key to the universe?

There is no reliable source for this quote. It has not been found in his verified writings or interviews, and fact-checkers found no trace of it before about 2018.

Why is Tesla linked to the number 3?

His biographer Margaret Cheney described habits built around threes, such as asking for 18 napkins because the number is divisible by three. Other stories, like walking around a block three times, are repeated widely but less well sourced.

Does the 369 method work if Tesla didn’t create it?

The method was never powered by Tesla. As a habit it can help you keep a goal in mind three times a day and notice progress, but there is no scientific evidence that it makes outside events happen by itself.
