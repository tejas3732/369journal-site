The 369 method for money: affirmations and how to do it

Money is the most common reason people try the 369 method. Here is how to word a money affirmation that feels believable, examples you can adapt, and how to turn the ritual into steady progress.

Updated November 2026 · By Tejas Rane, maker of 369 Journal

How to do the 369 method for money

  1. Choose one money goal. A raise, paying off a card, a savings target, your first client. One goal for the whole cycle.
  2. Write it as a present-tense affirmation, as if it is already true.
  3. Write it 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night, using exactly the same words each time.
  4. Repeat for 21, 33 or 45 days. 33 days is the most common choice for money goals.
  5. Take one small money action each day. Send the email, check the budget, ask the question.

How to word a money affirmation

  • Present tense: “I earn”, not “I will earn”.
  • Specific but believable: a clear amount works for some people; for others a feeling (“secure”, “at ease”) feels more honest. If a number makes you tense, use the feeling.
  • Positive: write what you want (“I am debt-free”), not what you want to avoid (“I am not in debt”).
  • Short: you will write it 18 times a day, so keep it to one line.
A useful test: read it out loud. If your body relaxes a little, it is the right sentence.

Pair it with one money action a day

Writing keeps the goal in front of you; action moves it. Pick one small step each day, such as checking a balance, cancelling an unused subscription, updating your CV, or sending one pitch. Many people log these in the evening, after the night session.

Does the 369 method work for money?

There is no scientific evidence that writing an affirmation brings money by itself. What it can do is keep your goal visible three times a day, which makes it easier to notice opportunities, stick to a plan and act on it. Use it as a focus ritual alongside practical steps, never instead of financial advice.

Questions

Can I use a specific amount of money in a 369 affirmation?

Yes. Many people write a specific amount, such as I earn $8,000 a month. If a number feels unbelievable or stressful, use a feeling instead, such as I feel secure and at ease with money.

How long should I do the 369 method for money?

Most people choose 33 days for money goals, but 21 or 45 days also work. Choose a length you can finish.

What if no money shows up after the 369 method?

There is no guarantee of results. Look back at what you noticed and did during the cycle, adjust your affirmation or goal, and pair the next cycle with one practical money action a day.

Can I manifest money and love at the same time?

Most people focus on one desire per cycle, so the 18 daily repetitions all point the same way. Finish your money cycle, then start a new one for love.
