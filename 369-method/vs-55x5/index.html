369 method vs 55x5 method: which should you choose?

Both are popular manifestation writing rituals, and both repeat one affirmation. The difference is rhythm: the 369 method spreads it across your day for weeks, while 55x5 packs it into five intense days.

Updated November 2026 · By Tejas Rane, maker of 369 Journal

How to do the 55x5 method

  1. Choose one desire and write it as a present-tense affirmation.
  2. Each day, write it 55 times in one sitting, staying focused on the feeling.
  3. Repeat for 5 days in a row, then let it go.

Which one should you choose?

  • Choose the 369 method if you want a gentle routine that fits around your day, or your desire is bigger and longer-term.
  • Choose 55x5 if you prefer one block of focused time and want a short, five-day commitment.
  • Struggling to stay consistent? The 369 method’s three short sessions are easier to keep with reminders.
  • Can you combine them? Some people do a 55x5 sprint first, then continue with a 369 cycle. Keep the same affirmation if you do.

Does either method work better?

There is no scientific evidence that either method makes outcomes happen, or that one works better than the other. Both are focus rituals. The best one is the one you will actually finish.

Questions

What is the difference between the 369 method and the 55x5 method?

The 369 method has you write one affirmation 18 times a day across morning, afternoon and night for 21, 33 or 45 days. The 55x5 method has you write it 55 times in one sitting each day for 5 days.

Is the 369 method or 55x5 better?

Neither is proven to work better. The 369 method suits people who want a gentle daily habit over several weeks; 55x5 suits people who prefer a short, intense five-day push.

Can I do the 369 method and 55x5 at the same time?

It is usually easier to do one at a time. Some people do a 55x5 sprint first and then continue with a 369 cycle using the same affirmation.

How long does the 55x5 method take each day?

Writing an affirmation 55 times usually takes about 20 to 30 minutes in one sitting.
