The 369 method: how to do it, step by step

The 369 method is a manifestation journaling ritual: you choose one desire and write it as an affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon and 9 times at night, every day for 21, 33 or 45 days.

How to do the 369 method

  1. Choose one desire. Pick one specific thing you want: a new job, a calmer mind, a loving relationship, a home. One desire for the whole cycle keeps your focus clear.
  2. Write it as a present-tense affirmation. Write it as if it is already true: “I am thriving in work that I love.” Keep it short enough to write many times.
  3. Morning: write it 3 times. Soon after you wake up, write the same sentence three times to set the tone for the day.
  4. Afternoon: write it 6 times. Come back to it in the middle of the day and write it six times.
  5. Night: write it 9 times. Before sleep, write it nine times and pause for a moment to imagine it as real.
  6. Repeat every day for your whole cycle. Use the same wording each time. That is 18 repetitions a day.

Tip: the hardest part is remembering the afternoon and night sessions. Set three reminders, one for each, at times you are usually free.

21, 33 or 45 days?

There is no single official length. These are the three most common, and there is no scientific evidence that one works better than another, so choose the one you can actually finish.

Common 369 method mistakes

  • Changing the wording every day. Keep the same sentence for the whole cycle.
  • Working on several desires at once. One at a time keeps your attention whole.
  • Writing in the future tense. “I will get” keeps it in the future. Write “I am” or “I have”.
  • Missing the afternoon session. It is the easiest one to forget. A reminder helps more than willpower.
  • Treating a missed day as failure. Pick it back up the next morning; consistency over time matters more than a perfect streak.
  • Only writing. Pair your affirmation with one small action toward it each day.

Where the 369 method comes from

The 369 method is often linked to the inventor Nikola Tesla, who is widely quoted as saying that the numbers 3, 6 and 9 hold a special key to the universe. There is no reliable record that Tesla said this or created a journaling method. The practice itself became popular through law of attraction communities and TikTok around 2020 and 2021.

Does the 369 method work?

There is no scientific evidence that writing an affirmation makes outside events happen on its own. What the practice can do is keep your goal in front of you three times a day, help you notice opportunities and small wins, and build a steady daily habit. Many people find that focus useful whatever they believe about manifestation.

Paper, Notes or an app?

Paper journals feel personal, but they fill up fast at 18 lines a day. The Notes app is always there, but it will not remind you or keep count. A dedicated app does both.
