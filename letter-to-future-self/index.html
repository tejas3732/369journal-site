How to write a letter to your future self

  1. Pick a date. One year is the most popular. Six months suits a goal you are working on now; three to five years suits big life changes.
  2. Write to yourself as a friend. Start with “Dear future me” and write the way you would talk to someone you care about.
  3. Describe today. Where you live, what a normal day looks like, what is on your mind. These details are what make the letter moving to reread.
  4. Say what you hope for. One or two things you are working towards, and why they matter to you.
  5. Ask a question. Something only your future self can answer, like “Did you take the course?” or “Are you still worrying about money?”
  6. Seal it. Print it and put it somewhere safe, or add it to your calendar so it comes back on the day you chose.

What to include

  • A snapshot of your life right now: home, work, friends, habits.
  • What you are proud of, even small things.
  • What you are working towards, and one step you are taking this month.
  • Something you hope you have let go of.
  • Advice or a reminder you think you will need.

A short example letter

Dear future me, it’s a rainy Sunday and I’m writing this at the kitchen table. Right now I’m proud that I finally started saving every month, even if it’s small. By the time you read this, I hope we have our own place and that we stopped saying yes to everything. Did you keep the morning walks? Whatever happened, remember that you were trying, and that counts. With love, me.

What to do when you open it

Read it slowly, then write a short reply. Notice what changed, what didn’t, and what you would tell the person who wrote it. Many people then write the next letter on the same day, so it becomes a yearly habit.

Questions

Is this letter to your future self tool free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account and no email address, and works in any browser on your phone or computer.

Is my letter private?

Yes. Your letter is written and kept in your own browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else. A draft is saved on this device so you can come back to it, and you can clear it with “Start over”.

How do I get the letter back without email?

Tap “Add a reminder to my calendar”. It downloads a calendar file with your whole letter inside an event on the date you chose. Open the file to add it to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.

How long should a letter to your future self be?

There is no rule. Most are between 200 and 600 words, about one or two pages. Answering three or four of the prompts is plenty.

When should I open a letter to my future self?

One year is the most common choice, often on New Year’s Day or a birthday. Six months works well for a current goal, and three to five years for big life changes.

What should I write in a letter to my future self?

Describe your life today, what you are proud of, what you are working towards, what you hope you have let go of, and a question you want answered. Specific details make it far more meaningful to reread.
