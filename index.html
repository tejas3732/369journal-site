Everything the 369 method needs, nothing it doesn’t.

One desire, one daily rhythm, and gentle help to keep going. No feed, no noise.

The ritual, counted for you

Each session asks you to type your desire the right number of times and shows how many are left. Simple guidance helps you phrase it in the present tense.

  • Morning 3, afternoon 6, night 9
  • After each session, see what’s left today and carry on in one tap
  • Your first 21-day cycle free; 33 and 45-day cycles with 369 Pro
  • Your own words, kept the same each session

Gentle reminders, three times a day

Most people stop the 369 method because they forget, not because they lose interest. Turn on reminders with one tap after your first session, or choose your own times.

  • One tap to set morning, afternoon and night
  • At most three a day, and they skip sessions you’ve already written
  • They go quiet if you take a break. No guilt trips

See your progress and the signs

A cycle map shows the days you completed. Log small wins and signs as they happen, and look back on how your practice is changing.

  • A free weekly insight after your first week
  • A recap at the end of each cycle, then choose 21, 33 or 45 days for the next
  • Past cycles are kept, so you can reread them any time
