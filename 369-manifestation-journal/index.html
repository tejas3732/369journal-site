How to keep a 369 manifestation journal, on paper or in an app

A 369 manifestation journal is where you write one affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night. Here is a page layout that works, what to write each session, and an honest look at paper versus an app.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Tejas Rane, maker of 369 Journal and author of two books on manifestation journaling

What goes in a 369 manifestation journal

Only four things matter:

  • One desire, chosen at the start and kept for the whole cycle.
  • One affirmation for it, short and in the present tense. See 50 examples if you need wording.
  • 18 lines a day: 3 in the morning, 6 in the afternoon, 9 at night, always with the same words.
  • A short note at night: one thing you noticed or did that points toward your desire.

That last note is the part most people skip, and it is the part that makes the journal feel useful after a few weeks, because you can look back and see progress.

How to set up the pages

  1. First page: write your desire, your affirmation, the start date and the number of days (21, 33 or 45).
  2. One page or spread per day: the date and day number at the top, then three labelled blocks: Morning (3 lines), Afternoon (6 lines), Night (9 lines).
  3. A small box at the bottom for the night note.
  4. A tracker at the front or back with one square per day to tick. Our free printable 33-day tracker does this for you.

A plain A5 notebook works well. Expect to fill around one page a day, so a 45-day cycle needs about 50 pages plus a few for notes.

What to write each session

SessionLinesTip
Morning3Write before checking your phone. Read the line once out loud.
Afternoon6Set a reminder; this is the session people miss most.
Night9Write slowly, then add one line about something you noticed today.

Write the affirmation exactly the same way every time. If you want to change the wording, finish the current cycle and start a new one.

21, 33 or 45 days

21 days is a gentle first cycle. 33 days is the most common choice. 45 days suits a bigger, slower goal. If you miss a day, carry on the next morning rather than starting again; the habit matters more than a perfect streak. The full step-by-step is in how to do the 369 method.

Questions

What is a 369 manifestation journal?

A notebook or app where you write one affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon and 9 times at night, every day for 21, 33 or 45 days.

Do I need a special journal for the 369 method?

No. Any notebook works. A guided workbook or an app simply saves you setting up pages, counting lines and remembering the afternoon session.

How many pages does a 369 journal need?

About one page a day, so roughly 25 pages for 21 days, 35 for 33 days and 50 for 45 days, plus a few for notes.

Can I keep my 369 journal on my phone?

Yes. A 369 app can remind you three times a day and count your lines. Some people write on paper and use the app only for reminders.

What should I write at night besides the 9 lines?

One short note about something you noticed or did today that points toward your desire. Over a cycle these notes show your progress.
