How to make a vision board in six steps

  1. Choose your focus. Pick three to five areas of life for the year ahead. Trying to cover everything makes the board blurry.
  2. Collect pictures and words. Use old magazines, your own photos, printouts or things you have drawn. Choose the ones that give you a small lift when you look at them.
  3. Print a template above. Or use a sheet of card. Print on ordinary paper, or on thicker paper if you plan to stick things on.
  4. Arrange before you glue. Lay everything out first. Move things around until it feels balanced, then stick it down.
  5. Add a title, a date and one sentence. Write something like “By December 2027” and one line in the present tense that sums up the year.
  6. Attach one small action to each picture. Next to a running shoe, write “Walk on Tuesday and Saturday”. A board with actions on it is a plan. A board without them is a mood.

Where to put your board

A board you never see cannot help you. Put it where your eyes land at least once a day:

  • Inside a wardrobe door or on the wall beside your bed. You see it first thing and last thing, when your mind is quiet.
  • Above your desk. Good for work and learning boards, and it keeps your goals close when you get stuck.
  • On the fridge or a bathroom mirror. Good for health habits and a daily glance.
  • As your phone lock screen. You will see it dozens of times a day.
  • Inside your journal. If your board feels private, tape it to the first page of your notebook and open it whenever you write.

Take a short look once a month. Tick what has moved, swap out what no longer fits, and write down the next small step. A vision board is allowed to change as you do.

Do vision boards work?

A vision board will not make things appear on its own, and we do not want to promise that it will. What it can do is keep your goals in front of you, and help you decide what you actually want. Researchers who study motivation have found that picturing only a perfect outcome can sometimes make people relax and do less. Pairing the picture with the obstacles you expect, and a plan for what you will do about them, tends to work better. That is why step six above asks for a small action beside each image.

Questions

Are these vision board templates free to print?

Yes. They are free, need no sign-up and run in your browser. Press Print or save as PDF and the sheet prints on its own, without the rest of the page.

What paper sizes do the templates fit?

A4 and US Letter, in portrait or landscape. Choose the size and direction in step 2, and the preview shows how the page will look.

How do I print a template without extra margins or headers?

In the print window, turn off Headers and footers, set the scale to 100% and choose the same paper size you picked here. To keep a copy, choose Save as PDF instead of a printer.

What should I put on a vision board?

Pictures, words and small details that show the life you want in the next year. Start with three to five areas such as career, money, health, love, home or learning, and choose images that make you feel something. Our 100 ideas above give you a place to start.

How many things should a vision board have?

Fewer than you think. Five to nine pictures and a few words are plenty. A crowded board is hard to read, and it is easier to keep the ones that matter in mind.

Do vision boards work?

A board will not make things happen by itself. It helps you decide what you want and keeps it in sight, and it works best when each picture has a small real action beside it.
