369 method FAQ

Quick answers about the 369 manifestation method and the 369 Journal app.

About the 369 method

What is the 369 method?

A manifestation journaling ritual: you write one desire as an affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon and 9 times at night, every day for 21, 33 or 45 days. Full step-by-step guide.

How long should I do the 369 method?

The most common lengths are 21, 33 and 45 days, and 33 days is the most widely shared. There is no evidence one length works better, so choose one you can finish.

What if I miss a day?

Pick it back up the next morning. Some people restart the count; others simply continue. Consistency over the whole cycle matters more than a perfect streak.

Can I manifest more than one thing?

Most people focus on one desire per cycle, because writing the same sentence 18 times a day keeps attention on it. Start a new cycle for the next desire. Need ideas? See 50 369 method examples.

Does the 369 method work?

There is no scientific evidence that writing an affirmation makes outside events happen by itself. The practice can help you stay focused on a goal, notice opportunities and build a daily habit.

About the 369 Journal app

Is there an app for the 369 method?

Yes. 369 Journal (369 Manifestation: Journal) for iPhone and iPad is built for the 369 method: it reminds you morning, afternoon and night, counts each repetition and tracks your cycle.

Is 369 Journal free?

Yes. The full 369 writing ritual, reminders and a 21-day cycle are free. 369 Pro is optional and adds 33 and 45-day cycles, voice affirmations, a vision board and an AI ritual coach. The annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, and a one-time lifetime purchase is available.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your journal stays on your device and there is no account. Nothing you write is shared unless you choose to use an AI feature or share a card. Read the privacy policy.

Is 369 Journal on Android?

Not at the moment. 369 Journal is available for iPhone and iPad.

How do I cancel the free trial?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose 369 Pro. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends and you will not be charged.
