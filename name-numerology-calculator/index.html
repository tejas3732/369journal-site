How name numerology works

Name numerology (sometimes called onomancy or the numerology of names) gives every letter a number from 1 to 9, adds up the letters in your name, and reduces the total to a single digit. Numerologists then read the meaning they traditionally link with that number. The idea is old and used in many forms; the version here is the Pythagorean one, the most common in the West.

You get three numbers from one name, depending on which letters you add:

  • Expression (or destiny) number: every letter in your full name. Traditionally linked with your natural talents and how you tend to go about things.
  • Soul urge (or heart’s desire) number: only the vowels. Traditionally linked with what you care about deep down.
  • Personality number: only the consonants. Traditionally linked with the first impression you give others.

One honest note: there is no scientific evidence that numbers predict personality or the future. Numerology is a traditional belief system, and the meanings here are what numerologists traditionally associate with each number. Use them as questions to think about, not as answers.

The Pythagorean letter chart

Write the letters from A to Z in rows of nine, starting again at 1 after I. That gives this chart:

ValueLetters
1A, J, S
2B, K, T
3C, L, U
4D, M, V
5E, N, W
6F, O, X
7G, P, Y
8H, Q, Z
9I, R

Vowels are A, E, I, O and U. This calculator counts Y as a consonant, so every number comes out the same way each time you use it.

A worked example: Maria Lopez

The letters are M, A, R, I, A, L, O, P, E, Z. Using the chart:

  • Expression (all letters): M(4) + A(1) + R(9) + I(9) + A(1) + L(3) + O(6) + P(7) + E(5) + Z(8) = 53, then 5 + 3 = 8, so 8.
  • Soul urge (vowels): A(1) + I(9) + A(1) + O(6) + E(5) = 22, so 22.
  • Personality (consonants): M(4) + R(9) + L(3) + P(7) + Z(8) = 31, then 3 + 1 = 4, so 4.

So “Maria Lopez” has an expression number 8, a soul urge number 22 and a personality number 4. The calculator above shows the same steps for any name, letter by letter.

Birth name, nickname or married name?

Traditionally, the full birth name (first, middle and last, as on your birth certificate) is the one that counts, because numerologists see it as the name you were given at the start. A nickname or a married name is often read as a layer on top: how you present yourself or the energy of a new chapter. Many people calculate both and notice where the numbers agree.

Some people feel strongly about one name and shrug at the other, and that is a perfectly good way to use it. The calculator above lets you enter a current name next to the birth name and shows the two side by side.

Master numbers in a name

If a total lands exactly on 11, 22 or 33, it is kept as a master number instead of being reduced. Numerologists often read 11 as a louder 2, 22 as a 4 with bigger vision, and 33 as a 6 with a wider reach. Expression, soul urge and personality are each calculated on their own, so you can get a master number in one and not the others.

Questions

How does name numerology work?

Each letter is given a value from 1 to 9 using a chart (A, J and S are 1; B, K and T are 2; and so on). You add the values for your name, reduce the total to a single digit (or keep 11, 22 or 33), and read the meaning numerologists traditionally link to that number. Different parts of the name give different numbers: all letters for expression, vowels for soul urge, consonants for personality.

What is the difference between the expression, soul urge and personality numbers?

The expression (or destiny) number uses every letter and is traditionally linked with your natural talents. The soul urge (or heart's desire) number uses only the vowels and is linked with what you care about at heart. The personality number uses only the consonants and is linked with the first impression you give.

Should I use my birth name or the name I go by now?

Traditional numerology uses the full name on your birth certificate, including middle names. Many people also try their current name, a nickname or a married name to see how it compares. Enter both above and the calculator shows them side by side. The birth name is usually treated as the main one.

Is Y a vowel or a consonant?

Numerologists disagree. Some count Y as a vowel when it makes a vowel sound, as in Lynn. To keep things simple and consistent, this calculator always counts Y as a consonant. If you want to test the other way, remove it and read the vowel number yourself using the letter chart on this page.

Why do other name numerology calculators give a different answer?

There are two main systems. This page uses the Pythagorean system (letters 1 to 9 in order), which is the most common in the West. The Chaldean system uses a different chart and often reads the final digit differently. Calculators also differ on master numbers and on whether to add each name separately first. Different answers do not mean one is wrong, only that the rules are different.

What are the master numbers 11, 22 and 33 in a name?

If a total lands on 11, 22 or 33 before it is reduced, this calculator keeps it as a master number, as most numerologists do. They describe 11 as a louder 2, 22 as a 4 with a bigger vision, and 33 as a 6 with a wider reach. 33 is the least commonly used.

Is name numerology real?

There is no scientific evidence that the letters in your name affect your personality or your future. It is a traditional belief system. Many people enjoy it as a prompt for reflection, and the meanings are a starting point for asking yourself questions.

Is my name saved?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Your name is never sent, saved or put in the page address.
