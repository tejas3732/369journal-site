    How to make a vision board that you’ll actually look at

    1. Pick one season of life. A vision board for the next year works better than one for “someday”.
    2. Choose 5 to 8 tiles, not 30. A few clear pictures are easier to remember than a crowded collage.
    3. Mix photos with short words. A photo shows the feeling; three or four words name it.
    4. Use your own photos when you can. A picture of your own street, desk or running shoes feels more real than a stock image.
    5. Put it where you’ll see it every day. Your phone’s lock screen is the place most people look at most often.

    Vision board ideas

    • Money: a savings number you’re working toward, a calm desk, “paid well for good work”.
    • Health: your trainers by the door, a full water bottle, “deep sleep”.
    • Love and friendship: a table set for friends, “kind, steady love”.
    • Career: the kind of work you want to make, “confident voice”.
    • Home and travel: the light you want in your home, one place you want to see.

    How to use it every day

    A vision board is a reminder, not a spell. It works best when you look at it on purpose for a few seconds and then do one small thing that points toward it. Many people pair it with a short written ritual such as the 369 method: one affirmation written 3, 6 and 9 times a day. Need words for yours? Try the free affirmation generator.

    Making one for the new year? Put your Word of the Year in the middle of the board, and use our 21-day New Year challenge for one small task a day to go with it.

    Questions

    Is this vision board maker really free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark you can't turn off and no paid version. You can download as many boards as you like.

    Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your photos are opened directly in your browser to draw the board and are never sent to a server. When you close the page, they're gone.

    What size should a vision board wallpaper be?

    The phone size here is 1179 by 2556 pixels, which fits current iPhones and most Android phones. The top of the board is left clear so the lock-screen clock doesn't cover your photos.

    Can I print my vision board?

    Yes. Choose the Print size for a 2:3 board, then use Print, or download the image and print it at any photo shop. It also works as a Pinterest pin.

    How do I set my vision board as my lock screen?

    Download it, or tap Save to Photos on iPhone. Then open the Photos app, tap the share button and choose Use as Wallpaper. On Android, open the image and choose Set as wallpaper.

    Do vision boards work?

    There's no evidence that a picture makes things happen by itself. What a vision board can do is keep your goals in front of you, which makes it easier to notice chances and act on them. Use it alongside small, real steps.
