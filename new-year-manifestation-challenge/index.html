A 21-Day New Year Manifestation Challenge for 2027

Most New Year goals are forgotten by mid-January. This challenge starts earlier and stays small: one desire, the 369 method every day, and one task a day for 21 days. Start on 11 December to finish on New Year’s Eve, or on 1 January to begin the year with it.

Updated 18 November 2026 · By Tejas Rane, maker of 369 Journal and author of two books on manifestation journaling

How the challenge works

  • One desire for 2027. Pick the one that would change your year most. Not sure where to start? Find your Word of the Year in five questions, then build your desire around it.
  • The 369 method every day: your affirmation 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night. New to it? Read how to do the 369 method.
  • One small task a day from the plan below. Most take five to ten minutes.
  • Two start dates: 11 to 31 December (finish on New Year’s Eve) or 1 to 21 January.

The 21-day plan

Week one is about choosing, week two about acting, and week three about looking back and preparing for the new year.

DayWeekTask (plus your 18 lines)
Day 1ChooseChoose one desire for 2027. Write it on the first page.
Day 2ChooseWord your affirmation: short, present tense, positive. Start 369 writing today.
Day 3ChooseRead the affirmation out loud. If your body tenses, soften the wording.
Day 4ChooseWrite three lines on why this desire matters to you.
Day 5ChooseTake the smallest possible first step, even five minutes.
Day 6ChooseNotice one thing today that points toward your desire. Note it at night.
Day 7ChooseWeek review: what felt easy, and which session did you miss?
Day 8ActList three real actions that would move you forward.
Day 9ActDo the smallest of the three.
Day 10ActWrite one short script of an ordinary moment after your desire comes true.
Day 11ActRemove one thing that gets in the way: an app, a habit, a clutter pile.
Day 12ActAsk one person for help, advice or an introduction.
Day 13ActA gentle day: do only your 18 lines and rest.
Day 14ActWeek review: what moved, even a little?
Day 15CloseRead back your night notes. Write down every small sign of progress.
Day 16CloseKeep the affirmation or adjust it now, ready for January.
Day 17CloseWrite five things from 2026 you are grateful for. Stuck? Use these gratitude prompts.
Day 18ClosePlan January's first week: when you will write and one action per week.
Day 19CloseScript the morning of 1 January 2027.
Day 20CloseWrite a short letter from your future self, one year from now.
Day 21CloseFinal day: review the 21 days and decide whether to carry on to 33 or 45.

New Year affirmations to start from

  • “2027 is the year I feel calm and confident in my work.”
  • “I am so grateful that I save money every month with ease.”
  • “I keep the promises I make to myself.”
  • “My home is peaceful, and I love coming back to it.”
  • “I move my body in ways I enjoy, and I feel strong.”

Need more? See 50 369 method examples, or the guides for money and love.

Tips for finishing

  • Set three reminders now, before day one. The afternoon session is the one most people miss.
  • Miss a day? Carry on. Don’t restart; the challenge is about coming back.
  • Keep the tasks small over the holidays. Five honest minutes beat an hour you skip.
  • Do it with a friend and send each other one line at night.

Doing the challenge on paper or in the app

On paper: any notebook works, and our free printable tracker helps you tick off each day. If you want guided pages, our two workbooks also make a thoughtful gift for someone starting the new year with an intention.

In the app: the 369 Journal app runs its own 21-day challenge on the same dates from 11 December, with a challenge card on the Today screen, reminders for all three sessions and a badge when you finish. You can follow this plan alongside it.

Questions

When should I start a New Year manifestation challenge?

Either 11 December, so the 21 days end on New Year’s Eve, or 1 January, so they end on 21 January. Starting in December means you begin the year with the habit already in place.

Is the New Year manifestation challenge free?

Yes. The plan on this page is free, and you can do it in any notebook. The 369 Journal app’s first 21-day cycle is also free.

What if I miss a day of the challenge?

Carry on the next day. Write your 18 lines and do that day’s task. There is no need to restart.

Can I manifest more than one thing in the challenge?

Choose one desire for these 21 days. One clear focus is easier to keep and to notice progress on. You can start a new cycle for the next desire afterwards.

What happens after the 21 days?

Review your night notes, then decide whether to continue to 33 or 45 days with the same desire or start a new cycle.
