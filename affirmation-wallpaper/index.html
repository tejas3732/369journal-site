How to set an affirmation wallpaper on iPhone

  1. Make your wallpaper above and tap Save to Photos (or Download wallpaper, then save it from Files).
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos and pick it.
  3. Pinch to position it if you like, then tap Add. Choose Set as Wallpaper Pair to use it on both screens.

Tip: you can link a Lock Screen to a Focus (for example Work or Sleep), so a calm affirmation shows up only at the times you want it.

How to set it on Android

  1. Download the Android size above. It saves to your Downloads or Photos.
  2. Long-press an empty part of your home screen and tap Wallpaper & style (the name varies by phone).
  3. Choose the image and set it for the lock screen, home screen or both.

How to word an affirmation for your lock screen

  • Present tense: “I am calm”, not “I will be calm”.
  • Positive: say what you want, not what you want to avoid.
  • Short: under 12 words reads at a glance. Long sentences shrink and get lost behind the clock.
  • Believable: if it makes you roll your eyes, soften it until it feels true enough.

Why put an affirmation on your wallpaper?

Most of us unlock our phone dozens of times a day. A short line on the lock screen turns some of those moments into a quick reminder of what you are working toward. It won’t change anything by itself, and we won’t pretend it does, but a reminder you can’t miss makes it easier to stay focused and take the next small step.

Pair it with the 369 method

Seeing an affirmation is one thing; writing it is another. With the 369 method you write the same line 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night. Using the same words on your wallpaper keeps it in front of you between sessions. Want photos too? Try the vision board maker.

Questions

Is the affirmation wallpaper maker free?

Yes. It is free, needs no sign-up, and the image is made in your browser. Your words are never uploaded or saved.

What size is the iPhone wallpaper?

The iPhone size is 1179 by 2556 pixels, which fits current iPhones sharply. There is also an Android size (1080 by 2400) and a desktop size (2560 by 1440).

Will the clock cover my affirmation?

No. The text sits below the area where the lock screen clock and date appear. The preview shows a sample clock so you can check before you download.

How do I save the wallpaper to Photos on iPhone?

Tap Save to Photos, then choose Save Image in the share sheet. If you only see Download, open the file from the Files app and share it to Photos.

Can I use my own affirmation?

Yes. Type any words you like, up to 140 characters. Short lines of under 12 words look best on a lock screen.

What should my affirmation say?

Something short, positive and in the present tense that feels believable, such as “I am calm, clear and steady.” Tap Inspire me for ideas, or use the affirmation generator.
