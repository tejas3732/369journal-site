A quiet place for one desire.

369 Journal is a small, independent iPhone app for people who practice the 369 manifestation method. It does one thing: help you write one desire 3, 6 and 9 times a day, and keep coming back to it.

Made by Tejas Rane Independent app developer · Since May 2026

Why 369 Journal exists

The 369 method is simple on paper: one desire, written three times in the morning, six in the afternoon and nine at night. In real life it is easy to drop. Most people try it in a notebook or the Notes app, and most stop within a few days.

Not because they stop believing. Because the afternoon session slips by, a notebook fills up at eighteen lines a day, and Notes never reminds you or keeps count.

The hardest part of the 369 method isn’t believing. It’s remembering the afternoon.

Most manifestation apps answered this by adding more: meditations, frequencies, feeds, coins, AI images. 369 Journal goes the other way. It is built around the ritual itself, done well, so that returning to your desire three times a day feels calm rather than like another task.

What we believe

1
One desire at a time
Writing one sentence eighteen times a day keeps your attention whole. The app is built around a single active desire for each cycle.
2
Private by default
Your journal stays on your device. There is no account, no profile and no social feed. Your desire is yours.
3
Gentle, never guilt
Reminders invite you back; they never shame you for a missed session. A missed day is a pause, not a failure.
4
The ritual stays free
Writing 3, 6 and 9 times a day, reminders and a full 21-day cycle are free. 369 Pro is optional extra support, and you can try it free first.
5
Honest about results
We will not promise that writing makes things happen. The 369 method can help you focus, notice opportunities and build a steady habit, and that is what we design for.

What we will never do

  • Sell your journal or use it for ads
  • Add a social feed
  • Send guilt-trip notifications
  • Promise guaranteed results
  • Lock the core ritual behind a paywall
