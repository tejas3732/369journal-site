Books for a Calm Manifestation Practice

Two guided workbooks from the maker of 369 Journal, for people who like to write on paper. Each has a short guide, then one page a day, with honest advice and no promised outcomes.

The 369 Method

A Practical Guide and 45-Day Manifestation Workbook

A short guide to the 369 method, then 45 guided days. Each day has a lesson, a question and room for all 18 lines.

Paperback on Amazon Kindle eBook

The Kindle edition has the full guide, a lesson for each week and all 45 daily questions, to use with your own notebook. The paperback adds a guided write-in page for every day.

  • Part one, the guide: the method on one page, where 369 really comes from, what it can’t do, choosing one desire, writing your sentence, planning your cycle and ten common mistakes.
  • Part two, the practice: 45 guided days. Each day has a short lesson, a question and room for all 18 lines.
  • Part three, the toolkit: what to do when you feel stuck, honest answers to common questions, a proof log and a plan for your next cycle.

Scripting Manifestation

A Calm Guide and 30-Day Workbook

How to write one ordinary moment of your desire as if it already happened. A short guide, then 30 days with a lesson, an example and a full page to write.

Paperback on Amazon Kindle eBook

The Kindle edition has the full guide and all 30 daily lessons, examples and prompts, to use with your own notebook. The paperback adds a ready-made write-in page for every day.

  • Part one, the guide: what scripting is, what it can and can’t do, and how to write a scene you can actually believe.
  • Part two, the practice: 30 days, each with a short lesson, an example script, a prompt and a full page to write.
  • Part three, the toolkit: help for doubt days, how to revise a script, and how to pair scripting with the 369 method.

New to scripting? Read our free guide, scripting manifestation, with examples.

Which book should I start with?

  • The 369 Method if you want a short daily ritual: one affirmation, 18 lines a day.
  • Scripting Manifestation if you prefer writing a short scene and picturing it clearly.
  • Both if you want to combine them: the 369 lines through the day and one script at night.

Paperback or Kindle?

The paperback is the full workbook, with a ready-made page for every day. The Kindle eBook has the guide and every daily lesson to read, and you write in your own notebook. Both are on Amazon; the links above take you to your own country’s store. In India, Brazil and Mexico, Amazon sells the Kindle edition only.

The app that goes with them

The books work on their own: you don’t need the app to use them. If you also want reminders morning, afternoon and night, the 369 Journal app is free to start on iPhone and iPad.

Reminders for all three sessions, every line counted, and a private journal on your phone. Free 21-day cycle.
