Which tool should you start with?

  • You don’t know what you want yet: start with the journal prompt generator and answer three prompts. Your answers usually point to one clear desire.
  • You know what you want but not how to say it: use the affirmation generator to turn it into one short, present-tense line.
  • You want to see it every day: put that line on your lock screen with the affirmation wallpaper maker, or add your photos with the vision board maker.
  • You want to look back later: write a letter to your future self and set a reminder to open it in six months or a year.
  • You keep seeing the same number: look it up in angel number meanings, or read what 369 means.

The tools help you find and word a goal. A daily habit keeps it in front of you. The 369 method is a simple one: write your line 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night, for 21, 33 or 45 days. Print the free tracker to tick off each session.
