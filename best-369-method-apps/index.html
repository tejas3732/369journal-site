Best 369 Method Apps for iPhone (2026), Honestly Compared

The right 369 app depends on what you want: a focused writing ritual, a strict 33-day challenge, a full manifestation toolkit, or something to listen to. Here is what each one is best for, with a direct App Store link for every app.

All six apps are free to download on iPhone and iPad; most offer optional paid upgrades. Feature summaries come from each app's own App Store description in November 2026. Features and prices change, so check each listing before you download.

1. 369 Manifestation: Journal (our app)

On your home screen it is simply called 369 Journal. It is built around one thing: choosing one desire and writing it 3 times in the morning, 6 times in the afternoon and 9 times at night. It counts every line, reminds you up to three times a day, and shows your progress across the cycle.

  • Free: your first 21-day cycle of 369 writing, gentle reminders (at most 3 a day, and they skip sessions you have already written), guided scripting, your first Breakthrough session, and a weekly insight after your first week. No account, no quiz and no ads. Your journal stays on your iPhone, with an optional Face ID lock.
  • 369 Pro (optional): 33 and 45-day cycles, the full Weekly Proof Report, unlimited Breakthrough Mode for doubt and overthinking, a vision board, voice affirmations in your own voice, future-self scenes, and daily ritual guidance. Pro is yearly with a 7-day free trial (we can remind you 2 days before it ends), or a one-time Lifetime purchase. There are no weekly plans.
  • Not as good for: Android users (iPhone and iPad only), or anyone who wants meditations, frequencies or a community.

See 369 Manifestation: Journal on the App Store →

2. 369 Method - Manifestation

A newer 369 app that turns the method into a structured 33-day program, with an affirmation builder, a day counter, reminders and a completion ceremony at the end.

  • Good for: people who want the classic 33-day structure laid out for them.
  • Consider: it uses a subscription with a trial, so check its listing for what stays free.

See 369 Method - Manifestation on the App Store →

3. Myla : Manifest & Vision Board

A broader manifestation app with a soft, journal-like design. It brings together vision boards, affirmations, the 369 method, meditation and Solfeggio frequencies, with hundreds of App Store ratings.

  • Good for: people who want several manifestation practices in one place.
  • Consider: 369 writing is one part of a bigger app rather than the main focus, and the full app is behind a subscription.

See Myla on the App Store →

4. WishLock Manifestation Journal

A 369 journal built around "locking" one wish: you write it in the present tense, picture a short scene of it already fulfilled, and practise it on a set schedule. It also suggests present-tense wording for your wish.

  • Good for: people who want help phrasing their affirmation and like a strict, one-wish focus.
  • Consider: it is very new, with few ratings so far.

See WishLock on the App Store →

5. 369 Manifestation & Meditation

An all-in-one toolkit that presents itself as an AI manifestation coach. It combines 369 writing (morning 3, noon 6, evening 9) with features such as AI vision boards, meditations, frequencies and breathwork.

  • Good for: people who want a large toolbox in one app.
  • Consider: if you only want the 369 ritual, a focused app will feel simpler. Check recent reviews before you pay for anything.

See 369 Manifestation & Meditation on the App Store →

6. ThinkUp-Daily Affirmations app

Not a 369 app, but a long-running, popular affirmation app for recording and listening to affirmations in your own voice with background music.

  • Good for: listening rather than writing. Some people pair it with a 369 writing app.

See ThinkUp on the App Store →

7. Paper journal or the Notes app

The original way to do the 369 method. Free and personal, but 18 lines a day fills a notebook fast, and nothing reminds you when the afternoon session comes round. Our free printable 33-day tracker helps if you prefer paper.

How to choose

  • You want the 369 ritual itself, done calmly and privately: 369 Manifestation: Journal.
  • You want a strict 33-day challenge format: 369 Method - Manifestation.
  • You want help wording one wish: WishLock.
  • You want many manifestation tools in one app: Myla or 369 Manifestation & Meditation.
  • You prefer listening to writing: ThinkUp.
