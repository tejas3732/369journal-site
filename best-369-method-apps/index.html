1. 369 Journal (369 Manifestation: Journal)

A dedicated 369 manifestation journal built around one thing: writing one desire 3 times in the morning, 6 in the afternoon and 9 at night. It counts every line, reminds you three times a day, and shows your progress on a cycle map.

  • Good: the full 369 writing ritual is free, including a 21-day cycle and reminders. Private, on-device journal with no account. Calm design with no feed.
  • Pro (optional): 33 and 45-day cycles, voice affirmations, vision board, AI ritual coach, grace days. The annual plan starts with a 7-day free trial; a one-time lifetime purchase is also available.
  • Not as good for: Android users (iPhone and iPad only), or anyone who wants meditations, frequencies or a community.

2. 369 Method – Manifestation

A 369 app organised as a guided 33-day challenge, with daily reminders, streak tracking, a vision board and affirmation recording.

  • Good for: people who want the classic 33-day structure laid out for them.
  • Consider: check its listing for how much is free after the first few days.

3. MYLA

A broader manifestation app that includes 369 affirmations alongside vision boards, meditations, audio and voice practice.

  • Good for: people who want several manifestation methods in one place.
  • Consider: 369 writing is one part of a bigger app rather than the main focus.

4. WishLock

A manifestation app with a guided 369 practice, nighttime visualization, progress tracking, and Lock Screen widgets and reminders.

  • Good for: people who want their desire in front of them on the Lock Screen.

5. 369 Manifestation & Meditation

An all-in-one spiritual toolkit that combines 369 writing with features such as AI vision boards, meditations, frequencies, breathwork and community.

  • Good for: people who want a large toolbox.
  • Consider: if you only want the 369 ritual, a focused app will feel simpler.

6. ThinkUp

Not a 369 app, but a popular affirmation app for recording and listening to affirmations in your own voice with background music.

  • Good for: listening rather than writing. Some people pair it with a 369 writing app.

7. Paper journal or the Notes app

The original way to do the 369 method. Free and personal, but 18 lines a day fills a notebook fast, and nothing reminds you when the afternoon session comes round. Our free printable 33-day tracker helps if you prefer paper.

How to choose

  • You want the 369 ritual itself, done calmly and privately: 369 Journal.
  • You want a strict 33-day challenge format: 369 Method – Manifestation.
  • You want many manifestation tools in one app: MYLA or 369 Manifestation & Meditation.
  • You prefer listening to writing: ThinkUp.
